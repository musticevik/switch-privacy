Questions, bug reports or purchase problems: email musticvk14@gmail.com. Please include your device model and what happened. We answer within a few days.

Common questions

I bought Unlimited Lives but it isn't active. Close and reopen the game while online; a purchase that was interrupted is delivered on the next launch. If it still doesn't show, email us with the date of purchase.

How do lives refill? Each session has 10 lives. A fresh session with all 10 starts 30 minutes after the last life is lost.

How do I change ad consent? In the game: Settings → Data & Privacy → Ad Consent. On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Why isn't my score on the leaderboard? Sign in to Game Center (iPhone) in iOS Settings. Runs continued with a rewarded ad are not submitted.
