Switch! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Agilersoft · Contact: agilersoft@gmail.com

This policy covers Switch! on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android (Google Play).

The short version

Switch! has no accounts of its own and no servers of its own. Your scores, lives and settings are stored on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK collects in order to show ads, what Apple or Google process when you choose to make an in-app purchase, and, if you are signed in to Game Center (or, once the Android version supports it, Google Play Games), your best scores sent to that service's leaderboards.

Data stored on your device

Your personal best scores, level progress, lives, the end time of any Unlimited Lives pass, and settings (sound, accessibility, and similar preferences) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. To make sure each purchase is credited exactly once, the app also keeps the purchase confirmation codes of your recent purchases on the device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We cannot see it, and there is no way to transfer it to us.

Notifications

If you allow it, Switch! sends one kind of notification: a reminder that your lives are back. It is a local notification, scheduled by your own device when you leave the game; no push service, server or identifier is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. The game asks for permission the first time you run out of lives. You can turn the reminder off in Settings → Refill Alerts inside the game, or in your device's notification settings.

Advertising

On both iOS and Android, Switch! shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (which you choose to watch, for example to get an extra life) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • a device identifier, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) or another app- or developer-scoped identifier
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (not precise) location of your device
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and other interactions with the app such as launches
  • performance and crash diagnostics

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob

In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is loaded. If you decline personalised ads, the app shows non-personalised ads instead. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Consent inside the game.

iPhone and iPad: App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to personalise ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not available to the app or to Google, and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device. The game plays exactly the same whichever you choose.

Android

On Android, you can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

In-app purchases

Switch! sells optional Unlimited Lives passes (1 hour, 3 hours or 1 week). On iPhone and iPad, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; on Android, by Google through Google Play. We never receive your name, email address, card number or any other payment details. We only learn, on your device, that a purchase succeeded, so the pass can be added to your game. Apple's and Google's handling of payment data is described in their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Leaderboards (Game Center and Google Play Games)

Switch! has two leaderboards, best score and best distance. On iPhone and iPad they are provided by Apple's Game Center. On Android they will be provided by Google Play Games Services when that is enabled in a future update; the current Android version has no leaderboards and sends nothing for them. If you are signed in to that service on your device, the game signs you in automatically and sends your best score and best distance from runs that did not use an ad-funded continue (a run continued by watching an ad is never sent), together with your Game Center or Play Games player identifier, to Apple or Google so they can be ranked against other players. Each time you sign in, those bests are sent again, to the account that is signed in at that moment, so a best set while offline still reaches the leaderboard. Your player name and profile are shown to other players according to your Game Center or Play Games privacy settings, which you control in your device or Play Games settings. We do not run a leaderboard server and do not receive this data; it is processed by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies (linked above). If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game works the same.

Children

Switch! is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children; it is rated 13+ on the App Store and offered to ages 13 and over on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we do not do

  • No account creation of our own, and no collection of names, emails, or contacts
  • No precise location collection
  • No access to photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • No access to your payment details
  • No selling of personal data

Data deletion

All game data lives on your device: uninstalling the app deletes it. For data collected by Google for advertising, see the Google links above; the in-game Ad Consent screen lets you withdraw consent at any time, and on iOS you can turn tracking off in your device settings. Your purchase history is kept by Apple or Google; you can view it in your App Store or Google Play account. Leaderboard entries are kept by Apple (Game Center) or Google (Play Games); you can manage or delete them from your Game Center or Play Games account settings.
